## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-impact corporate performance tailored for scale-driven B2B agency workflows, hyperlocal creator matchmaking, and marketing intelligence. It balances high-velocity corporate authority with vivid regional cultural storytelling.

### Target Audience & Tone
- **Audience:** Enterprise CMOs, regional brand managers, growth marketing directors, and creative strategists seeking measurable return on investment in localized markets.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, energetic, and metric-verified. The aesthetic rejects sterile tech minimalism in favor of commanding industrial typographic punch combined with luminous, electric data accents.

### Design Movement & Identity
The visual identity fuses **Modern Neo-Brutalist Typographic Impact** with **Precision Corporate Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-dense, compact uppercase headlines command attention and enforce scale.
- Crisp, air-filled neutral surfaces isolate content with editorial clarity.
- Frosted translucent pills, subtle high-precision boundary strokes (1px), and vibrant cyan data callouts guide the eye through structured analytical flows without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast kinetic blue accents balanced against deep obsidian slate, clean off-white canvases, and precise cool-gray boundary fills.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0088FF`):** Electric Kobalt. Serves as the core brand driver, focused action state, high-value keyword highlight, and active border accent.
- **Secondary (`#00C2FF`):** Vivid Cyan. Used for micro-badges, pill indicators, sparkline trails, and glow highlights against neutral containers.
- **Tertiary (`#0B111E`):** Deep Obsidian Navy. Drives display headlines, high-emphasis text, hero callouts, and dark-mode anchor surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool Slate. Regulates secondary copy, inactive icon states, borders, and structured divider lines.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` provides an illuminated, pristine backdrop that lets card containers lift naturally.
- **Surface Card / Container:** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle 1px stroke of `#E2E8F0` or `#BAE6FD` for highlighted active cards.
- **Data Callouts & Tags:** Soft tints (`#F0F9FF` background with `#0088FF` or `#0284C7` typography and icons) prevent visual fatigue while preserving scanning speed.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes high-compression condensed headline forms with fluid, ergonomic geometric body text and monospaced structural indicators.

### Font Pairings
- **Display & Headlines (`Anton`):** Heavy, condensed, all-caps presentation creates immediate editorial impact, evoking the energy of sports advertising, billboard presence, and industrial scale. Used exclusively in uppercase.
- **Body & Editorial Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Open, geometric, and clear. Mitigates the tension of the heavy display face by providing clean, effortless reading across paragraphs, subtitles, and lists.
- **Micro-Data & System Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Delivers technical credibility in pipeline blocks, sequence chips (`01`, `02`), SLA timers, and format matrix tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid system designed for symmetrical dashboard cards, metric grids, and dynamic agency showcase sections.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 3-column card groupings (4 columns each), outer margins of `3rem` (`space-xl` + `space-md`), and gutters of `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6 or 8 columns, 2-column card groupings, margins reduced to `2rem`, gutters at `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Single-column stack, outer canvas margin compressed to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), component gaps defaulting to `1rem` (`space-md`).

### Layout Structure
Cards maintain strict internal breathing room: `space-xl` (2.25rem) vertical outer padding on desktop to allow interactive mini-widgets (creator pills, workflows, reach velocity lines) ample separation from headlines and footer tags.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered structural borders and low-contrast ambient drops, eschewing heavy muddy drop-shadows.

### Layering Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an ultra-fine 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow is subtle and luminous: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 17, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Featured Focus):** Retains a `#FFFFFF` fill but features an electric stroke (`#00C2FF` or `#0088FF`) with a directional soft glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 194, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Embedded Modules & Wells:** Inset data regions (e.g., format matrices, workflow tracks, map points) use `#F1F5F9` or `#F8FAFC` surfaces with inner boundary lines (`#E2E8F0`) to create recessed, tactile utility wells.

## Shapes

The design uses a confident, contemporary roundedness profile (`roundedness: 2` — `0.5rem` base) to counter-balance the rigid, sharp lines of the condensed headline typography.

### Corner Radius Application
- **Standard Cards & Large Containers:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer boundary radius for a comfortable, modern hand-held or desktop card feel.
- **Sub-Widgets & Embedded Wells:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` to maintain nested geometric consistency.
- **Badges, Numeric Chips, and Interactive Buttons:** Fully rounded pill format (`9999px`) for quick tactile affordance and human-friendly scanning.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped with dark obsidian background (`#0B111E`) or electric blue (`#0088FF`), high-contrast white text, uppercase bold tracking, paired with diagonal arrow icons (`↗`). Hover triggers a smooth 2px lift and subtle glow.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline Action:** Pill-shaped, transparent fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, dark text, transforming to `#0088FF` border and font on hover.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Category Indicator Pills:** Ultra-small, uppercase tracking, soft pill container (`#FFFFFF` or `#F0F9FF`), 1px soft stroke, containing a colored circular state dot (e.g., `#00C2FF`).
- **Metric Verification Badges:** Micro-pills containing a cyan checkmark icon, `#E0F2FE` background, `#0284C7` bold text, and 4px padding.

### Feature & Capability Cards
- **Header Structure:** Top row features a left-aligned numeric counter tag (`01`, `02`) in a light-blue pill, paired with a right-aligned rounded utility icon container (`#F1F5F9` background, `#0B111E` glyph).
- **Typography Layout:** Large condensed uppercase title (`Anton`), followed by a 3-line descriptive body copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) in `#475569`.
- **Interactive Mid-Section (Dynamic Widget):** A dedicated data-well illustrating workflows, creator matching percentages, or audience verification curves.
- **Card Footer:** Horizontal flex-wrap row of feature tags (e.g., "District Geo-Fencing", "200+ Vetted Creators") styled as clean white pills with subtle cyan checkmarks.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Minimal white surfaces, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), focus state illuminates to `#0088FF` with a 3px matching translucent ring (`rgba(0, 136, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Toggles:** Rounded-sm (4px) check boxes with `#0088FF` active fill, sharp white custom tick, providing authoritative confirmation.
